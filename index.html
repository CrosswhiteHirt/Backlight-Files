The case

A blackout, a witness, and a missing piece of film.

Backlight Files is a cinematic, choice-driven thriller designed for mobile and tablet play. Read through atmospheric scenes, collect evidence, and make decisions that push the investigation into darker territory.

Cinematic scenes

Rain, city lights, camera footage, and quiet dread shape the world of the investigation.

Choice-driven mystery

Trust a witness, follow a coordinate, or challenge the data. Your route changes what you uncover.

Evidence files

Recover recordings, cards, drives, and fragments that connect the blackout to something larger.
